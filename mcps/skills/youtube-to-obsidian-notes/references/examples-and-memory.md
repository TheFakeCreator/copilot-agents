# Examples, Analogies & Mental Models

Meaningful examples are part of the explanation.

## Preserve

Preserve examples when they help the learner understand, distinguish, visualize, or remember a concept.

Especially preserve:

- real-world examples
- analogies
- metaphors
- demonstrations
- stories with explanatory value
- numerical examples
- code examples
- thought experiments
- counterexamples
- before/after comparisons
- memorable edge cases

## Keep examples adjacent

Place an example immediately after or alongside the concept it explains.

Do not move all examples into a distant "Examples" section if doing so weakens the learning connection.

## Preserve the strongest example

If the speaker gives several redundant examples:

1. Keep the clearest or most memorable example.
2. Keep additional examples if they reveal a different aspect.
3. Compress repetition when no new understanding is added.

Do not remove an example solely because it makes the note longer.

## Example labels

Use clear attribution:

- **Example from the video**
- **Analogy from the video**
- **Demonstration**
- **Additional example**

Assistant-generated material must never appear to be source material.

## Why examples matter

Examples can encode:

- intuition
- context
- boundary conditions
- contrast
- causal relationships
- practical application
- memory cues

A definition may tell the learner *what* something is; a good example often tells them *how to recognize it*.

## Mental models

Preserve useful mental models even when the speaker does not explicitly call them "mental models."

A mental model is a compact way of thinking about a system or concept.

Examples:

- "Think of X as..."
- "Imagine..."
- "It's like..."
- "The server behaves as if..."
- "Picture this..."
- "The key idea is to treat..."

Keep the model close to the concept.

## Do not over-interpret

Do not invent a deeper metaphor or philosophical interpretation and attribute it to the speaker.

If the assistant creates a useful mental model, label it as additional material.
