# Universal “Whole Spectrum” Prompt

> Explain the complete spectrum of [TOPIC].
>
> Cover:
>
> * the most positive perspectives
> * the most negative perspectives
> * neutral and nuanced viewpoints
> * historical context
> * major supporters and critics
> * strengths, weaknesses, contradictions, and controversies
> * how opinions changed over time
> * common myths vs reality
> * ethical, social, political, economic, and cultural angles
> * how different groups perceive it differently
> * both mainstream and unpopular opinions
>
> Do not give a one-sided answer.
> Avoid propaganda, blind praise, or blind hatred.
> Treat the topic as complex and multidimensional.
>
> I want the “grey spectrum” from black to white, including all major shades in between.

---

# Shorter Casual Version

> Give me the whole grey spectrum of [TOPIC] — from the best interpretations to the harshest criticisms, including nuances, contradictions, controversies, and balanced perspectives.

---

# Deep Research Version

> Analyze [TOPIC] across its full ideological, historical, ethical, practical, and cultural spectrum.
>
> Include:
>
> * strongest arguments for and against
> * real-world impact
> * hidden complexities
> * internal contradictions
> * evolution over time
> * misconceptions
> * expert and public opinion differences
> * why people strongly support or oppose it
>
> Present it like a spectrum, not a binary good/bad judgment.

---

# Examples

### For a person

> Give me the whole spectrum of Nikola Tesla.

### For a company

> Explain the full grey spectrum of [OpenAI](https://openai.com?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

### For a country

> Tell me the complete spectrum of North Korea.

### For a technology

> Explain the whole spectrum of Artificial Intelligence.

### For a religion/philosophy

> Analyze the full spectrum of Capitalism.

* “philosophical spectrum”
