# Add New Character Skill

## Overview

This skill creates a new character folder within your Worldbuilding/Characters/ directory with all template files needed to develop and track a character throughout your story.

## What Gets Created

When you use this skill, it will create:

```
Worldbuilding/Characters/[Character Name]/
├── [Character Name].md (main character profile)
├── Background.md
├── Personality & Traits.md
├── Goals & Motivations.md
├── Relationships.md
├── Character Arc.md
├── Appearance & Details.md
└── Voice & Speech.md
```

## How to Use

1. Provide the character's name
2. (Optional) Provide basic info: age, role, archetype
3. The skill creates all template files
4. Fill in each template as you develop the character

## Template File Descriptions

### [Character Name].md
Main character profile with quick reference information including:
- Name and aliases
- Age and gender
- Role in story (protagonist, antagonist, supporting, etc.)
- Status (alive, dead, etc.)
- Links to other characters and factions

### Background.md
Character history and origins:
- Childhood and family
- Key life events
- Education and training
- Trauma or defining moments
- Current situation and how they got here

### Personality & Traits.md
Character's nature and characteristics:
- Core personality traits
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Fears and phobias
- Quirks and habits
- Moral compass and values

### Goals & Motivations.md
What drives the character:
- Primary goal (story level)
- Secondary goals
- Why these goals matter to them
- What they're willing to sacrifice
- How goals evolve through the story

### Relationships.md
How character connects to others:
- Family relationships
- Friendships and allies
- Enemies and rivals
- Romantic interests
- Mentors and students

### Character Arc.md
Character development throughout story:
- Starting point (who they are)
- Key scenes that change them
- Mid-point realization
- Climax moment
- Ending point (who they've become)
- Character growth or tragedy

### Appearance & Details.md
Physical description and distinguishing features:
- Height, build, distinctive features
- Clothing style and preferences
- Scars, marks, or notable characteristics
- Mannerisms and body language
- How they present themselves

### Voice & Speech.md
How character speaks and communicates:
- Speech patterns and vocabulary
- Accent or dialect
- Favorite words or phrases
- What they're quiet about
- How their voice changes when emotional

## Notes

Templates are placeholder guides. Adapt them to your needs and story style.
